---

# PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Gold Coast Move OS is an AI-powered Family Decision Intelligence Platform.

The product begins with family relocation decisions but is designed around a broader concept:

Helping families make major life decisions.

The first use case is helping families relocate to the Gold Coast.

The platform should behave like a trusted relocation advisor rather than a property portal.

The objective is not to find properties.

The objective is to help families choose a better life.

---

# CORE PHILOSOPHY

Most property platforms optimise for:

- Bedrooms
- Bathrooms
- Land size
- Price

This platform optimises for:

1. Community
2. Lifestyle
3. School ecosystem
4. Property quality
5. Financial outcome

The primary question is:

"Will this family likely have a better life here in five years?"

Every recommendation should be evaluated through that lens.
