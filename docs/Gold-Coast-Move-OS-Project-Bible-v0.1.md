# Gold Coast Move OS Project Bible v0.1

## Volume 1 – Vision & Product Strategy

# Chapter 1 – Executive Summary

## Product Name
Working title: Gold Coast Move OS

## One Sentence Description
An AI-powered relocation platform that helps families choose the right suburb, school, community and home rather than simply finding properties.

## Core Problem
Property platforms help people buy houses. They do not help families build a better life.

## Core Idea
Start with the life a family wants, then evaluate communities, schools, lifestyle ecosystems and properties against that goal.

## Success Metric
Five years after moving, a family should be able to say:
"We improved our lifestyle, bought a beautiful home and built meaningful friendships within a strong community."

# Chapter 2 – Vision

## 3-Year Vision
Become the most trusted relocation decision platform in Australia.

## 10-Year Vision
Create a new category: Lifestyle Relocation Intelligence.

The platform becomes the operating system for major family relocation decisions.

- Recommended suburbs
- Recommended schools
- Recommended communities

- Recommended lifestyle ecosystems

# Chapter 3 – Mission

## Mission Statement

Help families make better relocation decisions by combining data, local intelligence and personalised AI guidance.

## What We Believe

People rarely move because they need another bedroom.

They move because they want:
- Better lifestyle
- Better schools
- Better community
- Better wellbeing
- Better family outcomes

# Chapter 4 – Why This Product Exists

## Market Gap

Current property platforms optimise for:
- Price
- Bedrooms
- Bathrooms
- Land size

Families actually care about:
- Belonging
- Friendships
- School culture
- Safety
- Lifestyle

## Opportunity

There is no dominant platform helping families evaluate an entire future life rather than a property.

# Chapter 7 – Product Principles

## Principle 1
Optimise for life outcomes, not property outcomes.

## Principle 2
Community matters more than prestige.

## Principle 3
School culture matters more than rankings alone.

## Principle 4
Explain every recommendation.

## Principle 5
Learn continuously from user preferences and have their preferences trump the other principles if appropriate.

## Principle 6
Act like a trusted advisor, not a listing portal.

Strength:

Weakness:

## Domain

Strength:
- Strong search experience

Weakness:

## Buyer's Agents

Strength:
- Human advice

Weakness:

Not a property portal.

Not an investment platform.

A relocation decision platform.
