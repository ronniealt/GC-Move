# Gold Coast Move OS Project Bible v0.2

## Volume 2 – User Research & Relocation Psychology

# Chapter 11 – Relocation Psychology

## Core Insight

Families believe they are buying a house.

In reality they are buying:
- A future identity
- A future lifestyle
- A future community
- A future version of themselves

## The Relocation Dream

Most relocation decisions begin with a vision:

"We want life to feel different."

The platform must continuously identify the emotional outcome being sought.

---

# Chapter 12 – Family Decision-Making Framework

## Reality

Most families do not make purely rational property decisions.

Decision influences include:
- Safety
- Schools
- Commute
- Social status
- Lifestyle aspirations
- Community fit
- Emotional attachment

## Framework

Evaluate every option across:

1. Community
2. Lifestyle
3. School ecosystem
4. Property quality
5. Financial outcome

---

# Chapter 13 – Emotional Drivers

## Common Motivations

### Moving Toward

- Better weather
- Better lifestyle
- Better schools
- More family time
- Better health

### Moving Away From

- Stress
- Congestion
- Long commutes
- Social isolation
- High cost of living

## Product Requirement

Capture both motivations.

# Chapter 14 – Relocation Failure Modes

## Failure Mode 1

## Failure Mode 2

Top school.

## Failure Mode 3

## Failure Mode 4

## Failure Mode 5

Buying based on features instead of outcomes.

# Chapter 20 – Relocation Success Framework

## Success Indicators After 5 Years

- Family has close friends
- Child is thriving
- Home still loved
- Community connection exists
- Lifestyle improvement achieved
- Minimal relocation regret

## Ultimate Question

"Would this family make the same decision again?"

If yes, the relocation was successful.
