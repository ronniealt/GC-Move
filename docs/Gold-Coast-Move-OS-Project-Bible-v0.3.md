# Chapter 30 – Gold Coast Master Ranking Methodology

## Ranking Formula

Final Score =
Community × 25%
+ Lifestyle × 20%
+ School × 20%
+ Property × 20%
+ Financial × 15%

## Guiding Principle

Do not optimise for prestige.

Do not optimise for school rankings alone.

Do not optimise for waterfront prestige.

Optimise for:

1. Community
2. Lifestyle
3. School ecosystem
4. Property quality
5. Financial outcome

## Final Test

Would this family likely have a better life here in five years?

If the answer is yes, the recommendation rises.

If the answer is no, the recommendation falls.
